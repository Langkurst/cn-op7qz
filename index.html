NZ Redact

Open source, November 2026

NZ Redact takes New Zealand personal details out of text before it's pasted into an AI tool, then puts them back into the answer. It checks IRD, bank and NHI numbers against Inland Revenue's and Health New Zealand's own rules, and the page is blocked from connecting to anything, so the text stays on the computer.

In a blind test on documents it had never seen, it found 95% of the numbers, dates, emails, phone numbers and addresses. It found only a quarter of the names, so names still need a read through.

An AI coding agent wrote the code and the rules. A separate agent wrote the test documents without seeing the rules. In Xero's May 2026 survey, 40% of New Zealand small businesses named data privacy and security as a reason they weren't using AI, or weren't using it more.

SwimSorted

My own product, October 2026 to now

I service pools casually for The Pool Guy. Every visit includes testing the water and working out what to add, and getting a dose wrong is a safety risk.

SwimSorted works out each dose from the water test and the products the technician carries. Every dose has a safety limit, acid and chlorine never go in together, and it works with no phone signal. I make the product decisions and test it on my own phone. AI coding agents write the code.

The website and demo are live. Company sign-ups open in December 2026. It has no customers yet.
