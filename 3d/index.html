How I work with AI

The AI drafts and recommends. It does not buy, publish, send or permanently delete without my say.

On SwimSorted, the AI twice recommended buying a domain before anyone had checked the name. I held off and asked for the check. It found the name registered as a trade mark in Australia, so I renamed the product.

On KeaClip, the AI advised adding cloud storage. I told it I must never be charged. It added hard limits, with tests, before the first upload.
