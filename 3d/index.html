06 How I work with AI

The AI drafts. I decide.

It does not buy, publish, send or permanently delete without my say. Passwords and keys stay out of logs.

Three times I stepped in

  1. A domain purchase I declined twice 15 October 2026

    The AI twice recommended buying a domain before anyone had checked the name. I asked for the check. The name was a registered trade mark in Australia, so I renamed the product.

  2. A cloud service that could have charged me 14 October 2026

    I told the AI I must never be charged. It added hard limits, with tests, before the first upload.

  3. Wording that read like slogans 7 November 2026

    I told the AI the business had to read as if I wrote it. The result was a rewrite, and a build check that fails if that style comes back.
