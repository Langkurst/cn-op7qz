Invoice checker

Matching supplier invoices to purchase orders was part of my job as a Purchaser at Häfele. This demo checks each bill against its order, line by line, and says what a person must look at before it is paid.

  • Price, quantity, lines, GST and totals are all checked.
  • Edit any number on the invoice and the result updates.
  • No AI inside. Plain rules with 29 automated tests.

    Made-up suppliers, orders and prices. Nothing is sent or stored. Sample data built November 2026.

    The rules

    • Order number and supplier must match the purchase order.
    • Price above the order price holds the bill. A lower price is a note.
    • Quantity above the order holds the bill. Fewer is a note, because the rest may come later.
    • Lines not on the order, or the same item twice, hold the bill. A missing line is a note.
    • GST must be 15% of the subtotal, to within one cent, and totals must add up.

    What it does not do

    • It does not check the goods receipt, so it is a two-way match, not a three-way match.
    • It does not read PDF invoices. The numbers are typed in.
    • Approve and Hold only change this page. Nothing is paid or sent.

    How it was built

    An AI coding agent wrote the code to my rules. Money is held in whole cents so the sums are exact, and 29 automated tests cover the rules and each sample.
