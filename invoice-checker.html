About this demo

Matching supplier invoices to purchase orders was part of my job as a Purchaser at Häfele. This demo matches each invoice to its purchase order line by line and checks it against Inland Revenue's tax invoice rules. It lists what a person needs to look at before the bill is paid.

It uses written rules, not AI. It runs in your browser, and nothing you type is sent anywhere. Approve and Hold only change this page.

How it checks

What holds a bill, and what is only a note
CheckHolds the billOnly a note
Order number and supplierEither one is different from the purchase order.Never
PriceThe invoice price is higher than the order price.The price is lower, or the difference is under the cents tolerance or inside the percent tolerance.
QuantityMore is invoiced than was ordered.Fewer is invoiced, as the rest may come later.
LinesAn extra line, or the same item on more than one line.A line on the order is not on the invoice.
A possible duplicateThe same supplier with the same invoice number, or the same total within 5 days.Never
GST and totalsGST is not 15% of the subtotal, to within a cent, or the totals do not add up.Never
Supplier's GST numberOver $200, the number is missing or fails the IRD check digit.Never
Invoice dateThe invoice does not show the date it was issued.Never
Your detailsOver $1,000, your business name or one identifier, such as an address or NZBN, is missing.Never

What it does not do

  • It does not check the goods receipt, so it is a two-way match, not a three-way match.
  • It does not read PDF invoices. The numbers are typed in.
  • Money is held in whole cents, so the sums are exact. 72 automated tests cover the rules, the extra features and every sample bill.
