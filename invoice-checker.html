    The rules

    What holds a bill, and what is only a note

    Against the order

    • Order number and supplier must match the purchase order.
    • A higher price holds the bill. A lower one is a note.
    • More than ordered holds the bill. Fewer is a note, as the rest may come later.
    • Extra or repeated lines hold the bill. A missing line is a note.
    • A possible duplicate holds the bill: the same supplier with the same invoice number, or the same total within 5 days.
    • Small price differences are a note when they are under the cents tolerance or inside the percent tolerance.

    Tax invoice rules

    • GST must be 15% of the subtotal, to within a cent, and the totals must add up.
    • Over $200: the supplier's GST number, which must pass the IRD check digit.
    • Over $1,000: your business name and one identifier, such as an address or NZBN.
    • Every invoice: the date it was issued.

    What it does not do

    • It does not check the goods receipt, so it is a two-way match, not a three-way match.
    • It does not read PDF invoices. The numbers are typed in.
    • Money is held in whole cents, so the sums are exact. 70 automated tests cover the rules, the extra features and every sample bill.
