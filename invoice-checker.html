Purchase order and invoice checker

Reconciling supplier invoices was part of my job as a Purchaser at Häfele New Zealand. This page compares a supplier invoice with its purchase order using plain rules. Pick a sample or change any number. The result updates as you type.

    The rules

    Check before paying a person must look at this first Note worth knowing, does not hold payment
    • Order number and supplier. The invoice must quote this order number and come from the supplier the order was placed with. If not, check before paying.
    • Price. A price above the order price is checked before paying. A lower price is a note.
    • Quantity. More invoiced than ordered is checked before paying. Fewer is a note, because the rest may come on a later invoice.
    • Lines. An item that was not on the order is checked before paying. So is the same item on two invoice lines. An ordered item that is missing from the invoice is a note.
    • GST. GST must be 15% of the subtotal, to within one cent.
    • Totals. The lines must add up to the subtotal. The subtotal plus GST must equal the total.

    What this demo does not do

    • It does not check that the goods arrived. A real payment check also looks at the goods receipt.
    • It does not read PDF invoices. The numbers are typed in.
    • It does not use AI. The same numbers always give the same result.
    • It does not send anything anywhere. It runs in your browser, and a refresh clears your changes.

    An AI coding agent wrote the code. Every rule above has automated tests. The suppliers, order numbers, items and prices are made up.
