What it finds

  • IRD numbers with Inland Revenue's check digit.
  • Bank accounts with the published bank checksums.
  • NHI numbers in both Health New Zealand formats, including the one from July 2027.
  • Card numbers that pass the card check, and driver licence and passport numbers next to those words.
  • Dates of birth, email addresses and NZ phone numbers.

What it does not do

  • It does not find names, street addresses or health details written in sentences. Read the redacted copy before you paste it.
  • It checks a bank account's checksum, not the bank branch register.
  • The expanded NHI rule follows the published description. It is not yet checked against Health New Zealand's full standard.

How it was built

An AI coding agent wrote the code to my rules. 22 automated tests cover the rules, including 6,000 cases checked against python-stdnum, an independent open-source library, and the six example NHI numbers Health New Zealand publishes. The example text uses sample numbers from official documentation.
