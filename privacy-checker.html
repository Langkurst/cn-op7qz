What it found

Kinds of detail to look for

    What it looks for

    • IRD numbers. 8 or 9 digits with Inland Revenue's check digit.
    • Bank accounts. Bank, branch, account and suffix with the published bank checksums.
    • NHI numbers. Both Health New Zealand formats: AAANNNC now, and AAANNAX from July 2027.
    • Card numbers. 13 to 19 digits that pass the card check.
    • Driver licence and passport numbers. 2 letters and 6 digits, only when the word licence, driver or passport is nearby.
    • Dates of birth, email addresses and NZ phone numbers.

    What it does not do

    • It does not find names, street addresses or health details written in sentences. Read the cleaned copy before you paste it.
    • It does not check a bank account against the bank branch register, only its checksum.
    • The expanded NHI rule follows the published description. I have not yet checked it against Health New Zealand's full standard.

    An AI coding agent wrote the code to my rules. The rules have 19 automated tests, including 6,000 cases checked against python-stdnum, an independent open-source library. The example text uses sample numbers from official documentation.
